Apply the Syncoria settings

Download Syncoria settings (Windows) ↓
  1. Open the downloaded Syncoria-Claude-Settings.reg file.
  2. Click Yes on the Windows prompts, then OK.
Had the old setup from earlier this year? Run this file anyway. It replaces the retired settings address.
Download Syncoria settings (Mac) ↓
  1. Open the downloaded file. macOS says a profile was downloaded.
  2. Open System Settings → General → Device Management (on older macOS: Privacy & Security → Profiles).
  3. Double-click Syncoria Workspace for Claude, click Install, and enter your Mac password.

Open Claude and sign in with your Syncoria AWS account

  1. Open Claude. If it asks "Apply settings from your organization?", click Allow.
  2. Click Sign in. Your browser opens the Syncoria AWS sign-in page. Sign in with your work account, then click Allow.
  3. Switch back to Claude. You'll see Syncoria Workspace at the top.

Check that the Syncoria skills arrived

The skills install by themselves within a few minutes of your first sign-in, and they update by themselves too.

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Plugins.
  2. Under Organization → syncoria-org, you should see syncoria-skills marked installed.
Not there yet? Quit Claude fully and reopen it. If it's still missing after that, see Help.

Part 2 · Connect your accounts

Connect the tools you use

Each person signs in with their own account, so Claude only sees what you can see. Connect only the ones you need; skip the rest.

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors.
  2. Click Connect next to a tool. Your browser opens. Sign in with your work account and approve.
  3. Back in Claude, the tool shows as connected.

Part 3 · Bring your claude.ai history

Import your conversations and projects

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Import & export and click Import…
  2. Click Sign in to claude.ai…, sign in in your browser, and click Authorize.
  3. Back in Claude, click Fetch export, then Continue.
  4. On Cowork & Code, click Continue (or Skip this step).
  5. Click Import. Your chats show in the sidebar, and your projects show as Spaces.
Want your uploaded files too? Use the file route instead: on claude.ai go to Settings → Privacy → Export data, download the zip from the email (the link lasts 24 hours), then choose Choose file… in step 2. Running the import again later won't create duplicates.

Part 4 · Plugins, marketplaces and extras

Browse plugins

  1. Open Customize → Plugins → Discover.
  2. Organization lists Syncoria's plugins. The other tabs list Anthropic's official marketplaces.
  3. Click a plugin, then Install. You can also add a marketplace of your own here.
Anthropic's marketplaces need Git for Windows on your computer. If they show an error, install it with the default options, then restart Claude. Get Git for Windows ↗
Anthropic's marketplaces need Git. If they show an error, macOS offers to install the "command line developer tools". Accept, then restart Claude.

Let Claude browse websites

The built-in browser is already on. Just ask, for example: "Open our Odoo pricing page and summarise what changed." Claude opens the site in its own browser window, and you can watch or take over.

Technical extras (optional)

  • Playwright browser automation: install Node.js LTS, then install syncoria-dev-connectors from Customize → Plugins → Organization.
  • Your own local MCP servers: Settings → Developer → Edit config.
  • Claude Code: the Code tab is on for anyone who wants it.

Help

Claude still shows the normal claude.ai sign-in

The settings didn't apply. Run Step 2 again, then quit Claude completely and reopen it. On Windows, quit from the tray icon, because closing the window isn't enough. If your PC also has company settings applied by IT for all users, the per-user file is ignored. Contact the Claude admin.

"Couldn't load your organization's configuration"

Check that you're online and can open this settings file. If it loads, restart Claude.

AWS sign-in fails or says I don't have access

Your account needs the ClaudeCodeDeveloper access in AWS. Open the AWS sign-in page. If you don't see the Syncoria account there, ask the Claude admin to add you.

A connector says "error" or "access blocked"

Click Disconnect, then Connect again with your work account. Google connectors need your account to be inside the Syncoria Google Workspace. If it keeps failing, send the connector's name and a screenshot to the Claude admin.

Import says export isn't allowed

Your claude.ai workspace owner must turn on Settings → Organization → Data and privacy → Allow members to export their own data on claude.ai.
