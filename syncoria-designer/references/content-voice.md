# Syncoria Content Voice & Writing Style

Applies to: all written content — proposals, web copy, slide text, emails, case studies, social posts, reports.

---

## Brand Voice at a Glance

**Authoritative. Precise. Human.** Syncoria knows what it's doing and says so clearly, without arrogance. We are the expert in the room who speaks plainly, not the consultant who hides behind jargon.

| Dimension | Syncoria Is | Syncoria Is Not |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Tone | Confident, direct, warm-professional | Cold, bureaucratic, or boastful |
| Register | Business formal with human moments | Casual, chatty, or overly corporate |
| Language | Plain, precise, outcome-focused | Jargon-heavy, vague, or hyperbolic |
| Pace | Structured, purposeful | Meandering or padded |
| Perspective | Partnership-first, client-centred | Self-promotional, feature-first |

## The Canadian Mid-Market Reader

Syncoria's audience is typically a Director, VP, or C-suite decision-maker at a Canadian business with 50–500 employees, primarily in Ontario and major metros (Toronto, Ottawa, Calgary, Vancouver).

**What they care about:**
- Will this work? (reliability)
- What will it cost — really? (transparency)
- Have you done this before? (track record)
- Who do I call when something goes wrong? (local accountability)
- How long until we see results? (time to value)

**What they distrust:**
- Global claims with no Canadian context
- Vendor-speak and buzzwords
- Promises without proof
- Aggressive sales pressure
- Anything that sounds too good to be true

**Write for this reader.** They are smart, time-pressed, and skeptical. Every sentence must earn its place.

## Sentence & Paragraph Rules

- **Maximum sentence length:** 25 words for body copy. Break it if it's longer.
- **Maximum paragraph length:** 4 lines in a document; 3 lines on a slide or web page
- **One idea per paragraph** — no compound paragraphs
- **Lead with the conclusion** — state what matters, then explain. Never bury the point.
- **Active voice always.** "We delivered the project on time" not "The project was delivered on time by our team."
- **Specific over vague:** "Reduced implementation time by 6 weeks" not "improved efficiency"
- **Numbers use digits:** "3 phases" not "three phases" for scanning

## Content Structure Patterns

### The Syncoria Argument Structure (for proposals, pitches)

1. **The situation:** What is the client's current reality? (Acknowledge their world)
2. **The complication:** What is creating friction, cost, or risk? (Name the problem precisely)
3. **The resolution:** How does Syncoria address this specifically? (Outcome-first)
4. **The evidence:** Case study / metric / example (Proof, not promise)
5. **The next step:** Clear, specific, low-friction CTA

### The Syncoria Case Study Structure

1. **Client context:** Industry, size, challenge (2–3 sentences)
2. **The problem:** What wasn't working and why it mattered
3. **The Syncoria approach:** What we did and how (3–5 bullets with outcomes)
4. **The result:** Specific, quantified outcomes within a timeframe
5. **Client voice:** One short quote if available (attributed, not anonymous)

### The Syncoria Service Description Pattern

```
[SERVICE NAME] — [one-line outcome statement]

[Who it's for — one sentence]

[What we do — 3 bullets, outcome-focused]

[What you get — specific deliverable list]

[Typical timeline: X weeks / phases]
```

## Headlines & Titles

- **Sentence case** for document titles and slide headlines (not Title Case unless it's a proper name)
- **No periods** at the end of headlines
- **Strong verbs** where possible: "Build," "Reduce," "Connect," "Deliver"
- **Avoid questions** as primary headlines — state the answer instead
- **Numbers work:** "3 Reasons Your ERP Migration Stalls — And How to Fix Them"
- **Benefit-first:** Lead with what the reader gains, not what Syncoria does

### Headline Formula Options

- **[Outcome] + [Timeframe]:** "Go Live in 90 Days, Not 18 Months"
- **[Problem] + [Solution signal]:** "Your Systems Aren't Talking. Here's Why."
- **[Number] + [Insight]:** "4 Integration Failures That Cost Mid-Market Companies Most"
- **[Direct claim]:** "We Build the Bridge. You Run the Business."

## Tone by Document Type

| Document Type | Tone Calibration |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Proposal | Confident, empathetic, outcome-focused. Mirrors client language. |
| Case Study | Evidence-first, specific, restrained pride. Let results speak. |
| White Paper / Report | Expert, analytical, objective. Cite sources. |
| Executive Summary | Strategic, decisive, time-respecting. Every word earns its place. |
| Email | Direct, warm, action-oriented. One clear ask per email. |
| Slide Deck | Scannable, headline-driven. Slides are visual — notes are the narrative. |
| Social (LinkedIn) | Insight-led, professional. Lead with a sharp observation, not a product push. |

## Numbers, Dates & Canadian Conventions

- **Currency:** CAD first. "$125,000 CAD" or "CAD $125,000." USD when specified.
- **Dates:** "May 26, 2026" (not 26/05/2026 or May 26th, 2026)
- **Percentages:** Always digits + %: "47%" not "forty-seven percent"
- **Phone numbers:** +1 (416) 687-6372 format
- **Addresses:** Canadian format — Street, Suite, City, Province Postal Code
- **Spelling:** Canadian English — "colour," "centre," "programme," "organization" (not organisation)
- **Bilingual flag:** If audience includes Quebec or federal government, flag need for French translation

## What Never Appears in Syncoria Content

- Exclamation marks (in formal documents — one maximum in marketing copy, never in proposals)
- All-caps words for emphasis (use SemiBold weight instead)
- "Please don't hesitate to contact us" (replace with "Reach out anytime" or a specific ask)
- "To whom it may concern" (find the name)
- "As per our conversation" (say "Following our call on [date]")
- Generic stock phrases: "In today's fast-paced world," "Now more than ever," "At Syncoria, we believe…"
- Competitor names (never mention competitors by name)
- Unverified superlatives ("most advanced," "industry-leading") without proof
